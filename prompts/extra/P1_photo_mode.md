Branch: lead/photo-mode. Doc: docs/integration/photo-mode.md.
Build a photo mode in the browser testbed (apps/testbed-web/src/photo/, new files; minimal hooks in main.ts): a key/button pauses the sim and enters a free camera (WASD/QE + mouse, gamepad sticks) limited to ~40 m from the player car and above ground; sliders for field of view, roll, exposure, depth-of-field-style blur (cheap), vignette, grain, colour filters that suit the low-poly dark look; hide HUD toggle; "Save image" renders at 2× resolution to a PNG the player can download (note: inside the claude.ai artifact viewer downloads are blocked — also offer "copy to clipboard"); works from live play and from replays (the replay system exists: wasm replay_*). No sim changes. e2e: enter, move, take a shot (non-empty PNG), exit returns to the same tick.
