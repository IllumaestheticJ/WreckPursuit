Goal: police that think ahead like a dispatcher: instead of every unit chasing the racer, contain the area the racer can reach.
1. Escape-region prediction: from the racer's position, speed, heading and the road graph, compute the set of road segments reachable in the next 20–60 s (time-expanded reachability with speed limits / racer's recent pace), weighted by the racer's likely goals (out of the district, toward tunnels/highways, away from units).
2. Chokepoint selection: graph cuts on that region — bridges, highway on-ramps, tunnel exits, district boundary junctions — ranked by how many escape routes they close per unit; assign free units to block the best ones (roadblock where doctrine allows, otherwise a unit parked across the lane), keep 1–2 units in direct pursuit, and re-plan every few seconds as the racer moves.
3. Search mode after losing the racer: a widening grid search of the predicted region, prioritising hiding spots (parking structures, alleys, under bridges), with units sweeping different sectors.
4. Doctrine limits by wanted level (L1 few units, no blocks; L2 soft blocks; L3+ full containment), identical in both damage modes; respect civilian-risk budget.
5. Debug view for the testbed captain/full map: draw the predicted region and assigned chokepoints (apps/ui-kit map overlay, small hook). Measure with lead/police-bench if available (capture rate vs the good/expert evader, escapes via chokepoints blocked) — tables before/after.
