Goal: police that drive like skilled drivers, not cautious robots. Scope is the low-level controller only (path in → controls out); tactics, targets and dispatch stay with machine E.
1. Path following on the lane graph with a racing-line offset through bends (apex cutting within the carriageway, using kerb-to-kerb width when in pursuit), lookahead scaled with speed.
2. Speed planning: from path curvature and the car's real grip (tyre/vehicle data, wet roads from weather) compute the max cornering speed and brake points ahead (forward-backward pass over the next ~250 m); trail-brake into corners, full throttle out; respect the car's power/brake limits; no "safe" speed caps during pursuit (lights on), legal speeds on patrol.
3. Stability: detect understeer/oversteer from slip angles and yaw rate and correct (lift, countersteer, brake modulation) like a skilled driver; recover from spins/crashes quickly (reverse out, realign); handle hills/crests (no launch-and-crash at crests: brake for blind crests).
4. Traffic/obstacle awareness at the controller level: short-horizon avoidance of cars and parked props on the path (swerve or brake), threading gaps; sirens make civilians yield (already in wp_world).
5. Measure with the police benchmark if lead/police-bench exists (merge origin/lead/police-bench); otherwise build a small lap/chase timing test: police vs an AI racer driving the same car around a Vesper route — the police driver should be within ~5 % of the race AI lap time, and crash into static geometry < 0.2/min.
